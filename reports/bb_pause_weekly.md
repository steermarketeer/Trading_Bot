# The middle-band hold, split by the weekly chart (six coins)

Each weekly reading is seen in the pump's direction; "yes" = the reading is true. W1 - W3 = the weekly points the same way as the pump (bullish weekly for a pump up, bearish for a pump down). **right** = the fair test (1.5 ATR either way; 50% = coin flip); **far** = the trader's target (hold: a new high above everything so far; fail: the sideways low). 90% intervals by a bootstrap of calendar months. Research 2020 - Oct 2025, holdout Nov 2025 on.
