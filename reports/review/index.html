What the bot saw (why it took the trade)
  • On the 4h chart, price ran above the recent highs and the 1,809 resistance: a liquidity-run breakout, scored 7 out of 10.
  • BTC's 4h trend was up and the 6h MACD was up. The path to the target had little volume in the way (the A+ condition).
  • The stop went 0.8% below entry, at 1,799.45, and the target at 2 : 1, 1,843.43, just under the 1,840 resistance.

What I think went wrong

  1. The stop sat right on top of a liquidity pool. On the 15m chart there were untaken lows at 1,794, 1,794 and 1,785, with the 1,785 support just under them. At 23:20 price dropped straight into that pool (low 1,778), took our stop on the way, and turned. The bot's "clean risk zone" check only looks between the entry and the stop, so it missed liquidity 5 to 15 points below the stop.
  2. We chased the move. At entry the 1h RSI was about 70, 1h volume was 5 times its average and price was at the upper Bollinger band on 15m and 1h. We bought after the push, not on a retest of 1,809.
  3. The daily chart was still in repair. Price sat on the daily EMA 50 (about 1,800), far below the EMA 200 (about 2,236), inside the 1,785–1,809 zone, with 1,840 resistance overhead.
  4. The idea was right. About 24 hours after stopping us out, on 13 July at 00:10 UTC, price reached our target. It later went to 1,900.

My suggestion for trades like this: put the stop below the pool and the 1,785 support (around 1,775), or wait for a retest of 1,809 and enter there. Both keep the same idea with a stop the market is less likely to hunt.
