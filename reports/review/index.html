What I think went wrong

  1. We bought the liquidity grab, not the breakout. The spike ran into the untaken 2,199.5 high, took the stops above it, and reversed. Our entry came at the top of that grab.
  2. No confirmation from the 1h or 4h, exactly your h013 note: the 1h candle closed back at 2,150 and the 4h candle at 2,127.
  3. The stop sat above liquidity again: the untaken low at 2,117 was 17 under our stop. Same as h039 and h013.
  4. The idea was right again: the target was hit three days later.
  5. Two positions on one move doubled the loss to −8.6%.

Your lessons, as they stand: (1) a 15m double top or lower high with a trendline break after a long = buyers are done: exit, or short to the liquidity below; (2) wait for the 1h / 4h close to confirm a breakout, then enter on the retest; (3) don't buy into a range top under falling highs; (4) one position per move; (5) no second try at a failed level. In 4 of 5 losses (h039, h013, h019, h029) the price took the liquidity just under our stop first.
