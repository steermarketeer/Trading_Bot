What I think went wrong

  1. This was the idea that was wrong, not only the stop. Unlike h039, price never came back. It fell 13% in 11 days.
  2. We bought into resistance at the top of a range, under a falling daily trend. On the daily, ETH had moved sideways between about 2,170 and 2,464 since mid-April, far below the EMA 200 (about 2,600), with the daily MACD turning down. The entry sat just under the 2,421 resistance and below the 2,464 range high. The target needed both to break.
  3. It was a stretched push. 15m RSI 75, price above the upper Bollinger band on 15m and 1h, 1h volume 4 times average.
  4. Your pattern from h039 appears again. After entry the 15m made a double top at 2,420 and broke its trendline, about 1.5 hours before the stop. That was the warning to get out (or turn short) before the loss.
  5. Three positions on one move made it −13.9% instead of about −4.6%. One position per move would have limited the damage.

Lessons so far (your h039 note + this trade): (1) a 15m double top and trendline break right after a long entry is an exit signal, and possibly a short to the liquidity below; (2) don't buy a breakout into resistance at the top of a daily range under the daily EMA 200; (3) one position per move.
