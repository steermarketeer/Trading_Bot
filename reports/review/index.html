What came before the entry
  • Daily: an uptrend (close above EMA 20 / 50 / 200). Since 17 Mar a range 1,709 – 1,860, with five daily lows at 1,709 – 1,722 (20, 21, 22, 24, 25 Mar): equal lows, a pool of sell-side liquidity.
  • 27 Mar, 14:00 – 16:00 (2h): 1,754 → 1,684, closing at 1,710.39 on 6.7× its 20-candle average volume (the day the US CFTC sued Binance). A 26-point lower wick: the low swept the equal lows and the 23 Mar swing low (1,684), and the close came back up to the range low.
  • The close was only 1.4 above the lowest daily low (1,709) and 1.6 below the 1,712 zone: 0.07 ATR beyond the level, not a decisive break close.

What price did
  1. 16:00 – 16:15: straight back up to 1,731.6, retesting the broken range. Our stop was taken at 16:05.
  2. 27 Mar daily close: 1,714.4, back above the 1,709 equal lows: the daily swept the lows and closed back inside (a failed breakdown).
  3. 28 Mar: 1,772 close. 4 – 5 Apr: 1,895, then 1,945: the top of the range and beyond.

My read

  1. The breaking candle was the sweep, not the break. It took the liquidity under the equal lows on climax volume, then closed back at the level with a long lower wick. The close was 0.07 ATR beyond the level; our own definition of a break needs 0.25 ATR. Nothing was left to fuel the short: the target was the low that had just been swept.
  2. Against the daily trend: the daily was in an uptrend and in a range. A first break of a daily range low, against the trend, is the classic trap.
  3. The stop sat inside the broken range (1,723, zone 1,724), right where the retest goes. It was taken in the first 10 minutes.
  4. The other side was the trade: the 27 Mar daily closed back above the equal lows (failed breakdown). A long at the 28 Mar open (1,714) with the stop under the 1,684 sweep low, aimed at the range top (1,860), was about 4.5R, reached on 4 Apr without the stop being touched.

The question for you: when a breaking candle sweeps the liquidity and closes back near the level with a long wick, is that a "no trade", or is it the long (after the daily close back inside)?
