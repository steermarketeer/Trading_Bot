What I think went wrong

  1. The same as h039: the stop sat just above liquidity. The untaken 15m low at 3,133 was 11 dollars under our stop. Price took it, took us, and turned. The idea was right; the target was hit a day and a half later.

  4. The liquidity told the story: the nearest pool was the low just under our stop; the big pool (short liquidations at 3,220 – 3,350) was above. Price took the small pool first, then the big one.

Pattern across the journal so far: h039 and h013 both had the stop a few dollars above an untaken 15m low, a 1h MACD bearish divergence, and an entry stretched above the Bollinger bands. Both hit the target later. h030 and h029 were different: real reversals at the top of a range.
