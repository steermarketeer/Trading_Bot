What I think went wrong (new, not covered by your lessons yet)

  1. The target was past three ceilings. 3,551 needed the 3,399 resistance, the daily EMA 200 (3,338) and the daily high 3,446 to break. The liquidity that was actually reachable sat right above us (3,380 – 3,430), and price did take it on day one. A target there (about 3,399) was hit, but it was only 0.66R: with this wide stop the trade wasn't worth taking.
  2. We bought the end of a vertical move. A 7.5% day, RSI 79 – 84 on 1h and 4h, above the upper Bollinger band on 1h, 4h and daily.
  3. Five days of no progress was the warning. After the 3,404 top, the 4h made lower highs under the daily EMA 200 and broke the 3,266 – 3,280 lows on 16 Jan. The trade could have been closed near the entry before the stop.

Possible new lessons: (6) put the target at the first big liquidity, and skip the trade if that gives less than 1.2 : 1; (7) don't buy right under the daily EMA 200 after a vertical day; (8) if the trade makes no progress in a few days and the 4h makes lower highs, get out.
