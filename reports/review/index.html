My read

  1. The target sat beyond the year's biggest liquidity, 4,099. Price went to that liquidity twice, swept it on 16 Dec and closed back below: that was the exit, and the short. The fixed 3R target was the problem (your lesson: not every trade has a fixed ratio).
  2. The closes gave the exit three times, each later and worse:
    · 16 Dec close back below 4,099 (sweep of the high): about +2.4R
    · 18 Dec close below the pullback low (failure to make a new high): about +1.0R
    · 19 Dec close below the 3,475 neckline (double top confirmed): about +0.2R.
  3. The short: from the 16 Dec close (3,985) to the liquidity below. Price reached 3,100 on 20 Dec, about 2.2× the risk to a stop above 4,110.
  4. The stop was again just above the liquidity (the 3,000 – 3,050 cluster and the 3,016 low). Both flushes, 20 Dec (3,100) and 13 Jan (2,900), closed back above the stop. The same pattern as 5 Dec 2023.

The question for you: at a known big top like 4,099, do you take profit just under it, or wait for the close back below (16 Dec) as the exit and the short?
