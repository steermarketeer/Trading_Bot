What I see as different (my guess)

  1. The pullback was quiet. After the top at 4,144 the 15m drifted down on falling volume. In h039 the drop came on a big red volume candle; in h029 one 5-minute candle had more than 10 times normal volume.
  2. It held support. The dips (lows 4,086 and 4,080) stayed inside the 4,071 – 4,110 support zones and between Fib 0.236 and 0.382 (4,101 – 4,074), above the rising 15m EMA 200.
  3. The daily trend was up. Price was far above the daily EMA 200 (about 3,409). h039, h030 and h029 were all below it. (Alone this didn't fix the rule, but together with 1 and 2 it might.)
  4. The bigger liquidity was above. Large short-liquidation clusters from 4,150 to 4,350, little long liquidation close below.
