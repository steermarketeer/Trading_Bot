What I think went wrong

  1. We bought near the top of a 4h range with falling highs. The 4h highs were 2,464 (17 Apr), 2,423 (22 Apr) and 2,404 (27 Apr): sellers came in lower each time. Entry was under the 2,392 resistance and the 2,404 high; the target needed 2,404, 2,421, 2,423 and the 2,464 high all to break.
  2. Stretched again. RSI 71 – 73 on 15m, 1h and 4h; price above the 1h upper Bollinger band.
  3. Your lesson 1 again, in a softer form. After entry the 15m made a lower high, lost its EMAs and trendline, and MACD turned down, hours before the stop. It's a lower high rather than an exact double top, but it's the same message: buyers were done.
  4. The stop was hunted, but a wider stop would not have saved it. The flush stopped just above the equal lows and bounced, yet the target was never reached; a stop under the equal lows (about 2,290) was hit on 7 May. The problem was the place we bought, not only the stop.
  5. Same week, same zone, same mistake as h030. h029 and h030 together cost about 18% of the account buying the top of the same range.

Lessons so far: (1) a 15m double top or lower high with a trendline break right after a long entry is an exit signal; (2) don't buy a breakout into resistance at the top of a range, especially under falling highs and below the daily EMA 200; (3) one position per move; (4) after a failed breakout at a level, don't buy the same level again days later.
