# Learning the indicators: every classic signal, the same fair test

At the signal close: does ETH move 1.5 ATR the way the signal says before 1.5 ATR against (30 candles max)? **A coin flip = 50%.** Bullish and mirrored bearish signals pooled. Research 2020 - Oct 2025, holdout Nov 2025 on. **chance it is noise**: the share of 400 time-shifted copies of the signal (same events moved to random dates, clustering kept) that are as far from a coin flip as the real one; below 0.01 = real.
